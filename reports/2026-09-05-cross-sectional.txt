READING IT

  Cross-sectional momentum has a higher mean terminal wealth than
  buy-and-hold and a lower median. That is the signature of a lottery
  ticket: it usually ends with less, occasionally ends with far more,
  and the 90th percentile carries the average. It ends richer in under
  40% of histories, at p = 0.19, with a median drawdown of -35% against
  buy-and-hold's -25%.

  It also wins only 8 of 25 parameter settings, so the single-path
  result that opened this file was the best cell of twenty-five.

  The row worth staring at is volatility_target: the best Sharpe in the
  project, and it ends with more money than buy-and-hold in 2% of
  histories. Ratio and dollars are different goals and this is what
  choosing the wrong one costs.

  Nothing here reliably ends richer than holding the six assets.
