  The gap between the first two lines is the whole result. Marking days
  near an earnings release does nothing; marking the right day does a
  great deal. Cadence lands within one day 56% of the time, and that is
  not accurate enough -- the variance is concentrated in a single
  session, so a three-day window spreads the correction over two days
  that did not need it and pays for the privilege.

  Comparison for scale: this project's return edge across eight national
  markets reached p = 0.145 and was called insufficient. The drawdown
  result reached p = 0.0039. This is p = 0.000031 with every stock
  individually significant, on information anyone can download for free.

  What it does not say: `experiments/tech_sector.py` shows the
  improvement does not turn into a better fund when it is expressed by
  holding less. A better forecast and a better strategy are different
  claims and only the first one is established here.
