READING IT

  Six ways to raise the top were tried. Predicting returns failed six
  times already. Better risk forecasting improves the ratio and not the
  money. Covariance-based construction does not beat its own diagonal.
  Adding asset classes made every strategy worse. Leverage works only at
  financing nobody is offered and only through 2008. Choosing higher-
  returning assets works and is indistinguishable from hindsight -- QQQ
  returned +14.84% and EFA +6.08% over this sample, and knowing that in
  2004 is the entire trick.

  What is left is the first table. The amount of money is set by how
  much stock is held, and every strategy here is a way of choosing a
  point on that line rather than a way of moving the line.
