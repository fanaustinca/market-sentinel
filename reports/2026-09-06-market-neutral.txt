  Breakeven near 20bps one-way. That is the whole result: this is not
  a question about whether the effect exists, but about whether it can
  be reached through the spread. It is why the effect survives at all --
  short-term reversal is payment for providing liquidity to forced
  sellers, and anyone crossing the spread to get it is paying the very
  premium they are trying to earn.

READING IT

  Short-term reversal is the only thing in this project to pass every
  structural check: stable across three decades, positive in 18 of 20
  parameter settings, and 6/6 on the Phase 1 null gate. Momentum and the
  low-volatility anomaly both fail outright in this form.

  It is also not obviously tradeable. Sharpe +0.34 at 5bps and zero at
  20bps, on a book turning over 25 times a year across 99 names, with a
  short side that must be borrowed. The edge and the cost of reaching it
  are the same order of magnitude, which is what an effect looks like
  when the market has already competed it down to the cost of capture.
