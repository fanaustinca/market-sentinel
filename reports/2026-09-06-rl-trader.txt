READING IT

  real data     Sharpe +0.889
  signal removed Sharpe +0.907
  fully invested Sharpe +0.920

  The agent is not broken: on a planted signal it recovers the feature
  at a correlation near 0.8. On real markets it lands where the
  signal-removed control lands, and both trail simply staying invested.

  Arm 1 says how far away the target is. RL needs a predictable
  component of roughly 25-50% of daily volatility before it reliably
  finds anything. The literature's estimate for daily equity
  predictability is under 5%, and this project measured its own
  supervised ceiling at 50.8% direction accuracy against a 52.6%
  base rate. RL is not the missing ingredient; the signal is.
