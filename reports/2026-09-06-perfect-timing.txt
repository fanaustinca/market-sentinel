READING IT

  Best out-of-sample accuracy: 50.9% on 240,740 training rows,
  against 52.6% for guessing 'up' every single day.

  Trained on perfect answers, the model cannot reproduce them on data
  it has not seen. Accuracy does not improve as the training set grows
  from 5,036 rows to 531,728 --
  which is the signature of information that is absent rather than
  merely hard to extract. More data cannot supply what is not there.

  This also bounds reinforcement learning. An RL agent must *discover*
  good actions through trial and error; this model was handed them and
  still could not generalise. RL cannot exceed its own answer key.
