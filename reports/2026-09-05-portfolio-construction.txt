READING IT

  The bootstrap edge is real and it is not a return edge. Volatility
  targeting beats holding on 90% of resampled histories, survives
  every change of asset universe tried, and needs no return forecast --
  which is why it succeeds where six previous attempts did not.

  But the period split is the number to read honestly. The whole edge
  is 2008: +0.308 through the crisis, +0.043 in the middle years, and
  -0.027 since 2018. It is crisis insurance, and the bootstrap scores
  it well because it resamples a history that contains a crisis.

  Out of sample across eight national indices the edge falls to +0.041
  and stops being significant (6/8 markets, p = 0.14) -- while drawdown
  improves in 8 of 8. That is the same sentence this project has
  written from six directions now: the risk result replicates, the
  return result does not.
